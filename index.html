01

Natural Language Filter

Letting people ask for the records they need in plain language, instead of stacking filters across menus.

Lead designer Query & data structure Intapp · 2024
Problem
Users had to stack multiple filters across several menus. Even a simple question — which Tier A clients haven't been contacted in the last four months? — meant digging through dropdowns, picking the right fields, and understanding internal hierarchies. For non-technical users that was too much friction.
Insight
People should be able to find relevant data without knowing the exact field names or the data structure. The work was bridging a structured enterprise system with the way people naturally think and ask questions.
Impact
10 of 10 internal testers said they would use it over the standard filter.
02

AI Assisted Intake Form

Drop in an email and the form fills itself — every suggestion reviewable inline, so control never leaves the user.

Lead designer Human-in-the-loop AI Intapp · 2024
Problem
Intake forms were long and repetitive — users re-typed information already written in emails, then double-checked it for accuracy.
Insight
The hard question was not technical. It was how much of the AI users should see: a magic autofill moment, or a collaborative partner. Testing settled it — people skimmed the summary panel and still asked which fields had changed.
Design
Suggestions live beside the fields they fill. A toggle highlights everything AI touched, and where several values compete, the user picks without leaving the form.
Impact
Email ingestion removes roughly 10% of the ~200 fields a user would otherwise fill by hand.

AI Applied Outreach

Turning the relationship intelligence already sitting in DealCloud into personalized client outreach, generated in a single click.

Lead designer Agentive workflow Intapp · 2023
The problem
